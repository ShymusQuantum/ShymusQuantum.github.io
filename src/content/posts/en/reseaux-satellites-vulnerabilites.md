This report is the first in a series I'm dedicating to the security of space systems. It lays the
foundations: how a communication leaves a satellite, crosses thousands of kilometres of vacuum, and
comes back down to us — and, above all, where along that whole chain an attacker can slip in.

I start from the waves themselves, then work my way up through the entire architecture: the components
of a satellite, the RF chains, VSAT networks, low-Earth-orbit constellations, all the way to encryption
mechanisms and the first building blocks of quantum cryptography. The angle is deliberately a cyber one:
every component that adds performance also adds attack surface, and that is the thread I pull from one
end of the document to the other.
