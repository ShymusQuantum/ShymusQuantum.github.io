This blog is my logbook. It is where I keep what I learn about cybersecurity, software-defined radio
and satellites: experiments, CTF writeups and research notes.

## what you will find here

- **satellite reception**: weather satellites, cubesats, telemetry;
- **space systems security**: ground stations, links, protocols;
- **writeups** of CTFs and research notes.

## under the hood

The site is generated with [Astro](https://astro.build) and hosted for free on GitHub Pages.
The background is a real photo of the ALMA antennas under the Milky Way (ESO). The satellites
crossing the sky are animated live in your browser.
